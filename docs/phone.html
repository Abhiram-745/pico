Pico on your phone.

There's nothing to download. Your laptop serves the app, so you always get the version it's running.

Pair

  1. On your laptop, run node bridge/server.mjs.
  2. A QR code appears, with a pairing code under it.
  3. Scan it with your camera, on the same Wi-Fi. That's it — it remembers.

Keep it on your home screen

iPhone

Open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. You get a real full-screen app.

Android

Open the ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen.

Chrome saves its full Install for sites on trusted HTTPS, which a local address like 10.0.0.7 can't have. Everything works the same either way; if you want the real install, serve the bridge through Tailscale Serve, which gives it a proper certificate.

What it can do

Send tasks
Type what you want done and watch each step as it happens.
Answer approvals
Allow or cancel a consequential action from your pocket. It can answer one, never skip one.
Pause and stop
Halt a run without going back to the laptop.
Stay on your network
Your phone connects straight to your laptop. No relay, no account, nothing internet-facing.

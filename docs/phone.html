Pico on your phone

There is no app store download. The phone app is served by your own laptop, and installs straight from the browser.

Set it up 3 minutes

  1. On your laptop, open a terminal in the Pico folder and run node bridge/server.mjs.
  2. A QR code appears in the terminal, with a pairing code beneath it.
  3. Point your phone camera at it, on the same Wi-Fi. Tap the link.
  4. Pico opens and pairs itself. You only do this once — it remembers.

Add to home screen Android

  1. Tap Install when Pico offers it, or open the ⋮ menu.
  2. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Add to home screen iPhone

  1. Open the link in Safari (not Chrome).
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Why not an .apk? Pico is served by your own laptop over your own Wi-Fi, so there is nothing to sideload and nothing to keep updated — you always get the version your laptop is running. Installing it this way works on iPhone too, which an Android package never would.

Nothing leaves your network. Your phone connects straight to your laptop's local address. There is no relay, no account, and no cloud service in between. Approvals and takeovers still happen — the phone can answer them, never skip them.
